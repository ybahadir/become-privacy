Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy covers the iOS app be - become. and its widget, maintained by the developer using the GitHub account ybahadir ("we").

Your writing, recordings and selected photos are stored on your device. The app does not require an account, upload this content to our servers, use advertising trackers or include a third-party analytics SDK.

1. Information used on your device

The app stores the information you choose to enter: your name, onboarding answers, topics, intentions, journal entries and drafts, practice history, affirmation favorites and history, board content, voice recordings and selected photos. It also stores preferences such as appearance, language and reminder times, recording usage limits, and local feature-use counters. These counters stay on the device and are not transmitted to us.

This information is used to provide your journal, personalize your practice, display your saved content, remember preferences and schedule reminders. A small local app-group storage area shares affirmation text, appearance, language and membership status with the widget. Journal entries, recordings and photos are not shared with the widget. We do not operate an account service or a cloud sync service for this content.

2. Permissions

  • Microphone: used with your permission when you record your voice. Recordings are saved locally.
  • Photos: the system photo picker provides only the images you choose to import. The app stores a local copy for the selected feature.
  • Notifications: optional reminders are scheduled on your device with your permission.

You can turn off microphone or notification permissions in iOS Settings. You can remove imported images and recordings in the app. Withdrawing a permission prevents future use of that permission; it does not automatically delete existing content.

3. Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes subscription purchases and payments through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to unlock features and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may process purchase, diagnostic and backup data according to your settings and its Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing, export and backups

We do not sell your personal information or track you across other companies' apps or websites. Content leaves the app when you explicitly export or share it using the system share sheet, or when iOS includes app data in a device backup according to your settings. Any app, recipient or storage provider you choose handles the shared copy under its own practices. This app does not control those copies or Apple's backups.

5. Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it or remove the app's data. Open You → Manage your data → Delete all personal data to remove personal content, imported media, reminders and local personal preferences. You can also use Export written data to save a text copy of your writing; that export does not include photos or voice recordings.

Deleting the app (rather than offloading it) removes its app-local data through iOS. Backups and previously exported or shared copies must be managed separately. Deleting data does not cancel an App Store subscription or delete Apple's purchase records; manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete journal content stored only on your device.

6. This website and privacy requests

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising or cookies to this page. GitHub may process technical visitor data, including IP addresses and request logs, as described in its Privacy Statement.

For privacy questions or requests, use the privacy repository's Issues page. Issues are public and require a GitHub account to submit; do not include journal content, recordings, payment details or other sensitive information. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond. Public messages remain on GitHub until removed, subject to GitHub's retention practices. You may request removal of content we control through the same contact channel.

7. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if the app's privacy practices change.
